A note from Anurag

Priyanshi,

I have spent a year watching you do the hardest thing I have ever seen anyone do, and do it as though it were obvious.

I want to be honest about something. Before Adira came, I thought I already knew how much I admired you. I didn't. I had no way to. You can't know how someone will be at three in the morning in the eleventh month until you have watched them be exactly as gentle then as they were on the first night.

What I keep thinking about is how much of it never showed. You carried her through airports and let everyone believe it was easy. You took her to Thailand at five months old when most people would have waited, and worked the whole week so she could have a holiday. You were tired in a way I couldn't fix, for months, and she never once felt it. She has no idea. That was the point — that was the whole design, and you built it.

She turned one in Paris, walking half a step at a time, delighted with herself and entirely unaware that anything had ever been difficult. That is the best thing anyone has ever made, and you made it.

Thank you for her. Thank you for being the kind of mother I get to watch and learn from. And thank you for still being Priyanshi through all of it — the person I chose, who was already the best thing that had happened to me long before any of this started.

Happy birthday. I love you.

Anurag
